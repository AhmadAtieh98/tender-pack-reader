## 2. The workflow

`tenderpack ai run ADD-NN --pdf PATH --route host|recorded|anthropic|openrouter|ollama [--host-model-alias opus] [--batch-size 8] [--stop-after STEP]`, `ai resume RUN_ID`, `ai submit-batch RUN_ID FILE --by NAME` (a coding assistant without MCP), `ai run-status RUN_ID`.

ingest (a disposable candidate workspace under `staging/ai/runs/<run_id>/candidate/`: the curation, config, assumptions, templates, clarification register and relationships copied with their hashes; the PDF added; `out-before/` built once as the last validated state) → analysis in bounded batches (every provision listed in the checkpoint; pending → proposed → validated or unaccounted) → deterministic validation → downstream proposals (row_reading, row_new, issue, clarification_item, evidence_item, activity, dependency) → downstream validation in the candidate (quotes verbatim at the stage, consequence classes, date rules, ids, `clarify.check`, the schedule checks, `relationships.validate`; interactions between ops and dependent proposals) → promotion into the candidate only → pin, check-register, outputs (banners; A1's status column: proposed / unresolved / approved), diff → review packet with each provision's chain (source evidence → proposed transition → validation → downstream impact → output difference), the escalations first, timings per step, every manual intervention. Unknown change types escalate with their evidence and affected scope. A refused build keeps `out-before/`. Resumption never re-asks a done provision and never duplicates one.

Measured: the recorded route end to end in 3.7 min (partial by design); the host route on blind-03's addendum in 12.7 min (default host model; 35/35 provisions reached validation; most downstream items failed validation because the host wrote free text where statement ids were expected — the schema and instructions were then clarified). The real `curation/`, `config/` and `out/` are byte-compared before and after a run in the tests.

## 7. Remaining limitations

1. **Timing.** The workflow took 45.6 min on blind-04 against the 30-minute target; the host sessions (3–8 min each on the Opus alias, 7 of them) are the cost. Batches could run concurrently on a plan without a rate limit; this one answered 429 after seven sessions. Nothing was weakened to meet the target.
2. **The downstream phase on the host route** did not deliver on blind-04: its sessions failed on the rate limit, and on W3's blind-03 exercise most of its items failed validation (free text where ids were expected). The rows, readings, issues, activities and dependencies a curator wrote by hand in blind-03 are still, in practice, a person's work; the contract, validation and promotion paths for them exist and are tested with recorded cassettes.
3. **Derived quantities are not computed** (a value expressed as a percentage of another, a cap in days, a threshold as a percentage of a total, a pipe bore from a velocity): `calculate` has no purpose for them, so the chains that run through them are not followed.
4. **Relationships are curated per pack.** The 22 entries cover the blind-03 gaps; blind-04's new chains (velocity → bore → crossings; EPC definition → LD, cap, net-worth test) had no entry, and the AI's dependency proposals (which would add proposed ones) failed with the downstream phase.
5. **Two citation/engine gaps found by blind-04 are fixed post-key** (tables issued by an addendum; cover verbs); a direction-reversal claim ("relaxes" on a lowered ceiling) still needs the model's judgement (a critic prompt on cover claims about direction is a follow-up).
6. **No live API execution** (no key); structured outputs, capability verification against a real endpoint, cost, and the OpenRouter and Ollama routes are recorded-tested only. The host's model is declared by the CLI, not verified by the application.
7. **A confirming answer reads as a change** in `diff` and the STALE logic when its annotation adds a dependency (VOL-IV-F4D-01 in blind-04).
8. **Conditional amendments** have no op shape of their own (trigger, deadline, two states), so A5 cannot plan a decision milestone for them; they are escalated.
9. **The candidate is PARTIAL** whenever any provision is unresolved, so A3 and the A5 programme show the previous validated state; the proposals are visible in A1's status column, A2, `a5/working/` and the diff. This is by design (the last validated state is preserved), but it means a 24-unresolved candidate shows little on A3/A5.
10. **Human review time is estimated**, not measured; the rehearsals' outputs were not reviewed by a person.
11. **Checked on Linux x86_64 only;** the Mac, native viewers and every live route are unchecked. The offline archive was not rebuilt in session 10 (the commit came at its very end).
